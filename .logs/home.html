MOVE BY MOVE

Chess, without the admin.

01 — Pairings

Swiss, seeded by score.

Players meet the nearest score they have not already played. No repeat pairings, no manual bracket.

  • No rematchesThe draw backtracks until no board is a repeat.
  • Byes to the bottomThe lowest score without one sits out.
  • Colours balancedA third colour in a row is refused when it can be avoided.

02 — Time controls

1410123456789401234567890123456789presets

From one minute with no increment to a day per move. The bank and the Fischer bonus are enforced on the server, per game.

  • Bullet
  • Blitz
  • Rapid
  • Classical
  • Correspondence
  • Untimed

03 — Clocks

Server time, not vibes.

Every move is timestamped and validated against the bank. A client that lies about its clock loses on time.

04 — Review

Stockfish on every game.

Finish a game and the analysis is one click. Blunders are marked where they were played.
